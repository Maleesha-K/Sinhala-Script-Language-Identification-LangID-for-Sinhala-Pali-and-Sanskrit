## 6. Challenges & Learnings Slide (Rubric: Professionalism & Viva)

Directly mirroring **Senior Presentation 2 (Slides 52–53)**, evaluators award maximum marks when students show genuine self-reflection:

### **Slide 41: Challenges & Key Learnings**
- **Challenge 1: The Catastrophic Forgetting Barrier:**
  - *Problem:* Initial fine-tuning caused English F1 to collapse from 0.938 to 0.707.
  - *Resolution:* Shifted from brute-force fine-tuning to Hierarchical Softmax Leaf Surgery and memory rehearsal.
- **Challenge 2: Archaic Ligatures & Zero-Width Joiners:**
  - *Problem:* Sanskrit and Pali texts utilize complex conjunct consonants (bandi akuru) causing token fragmentation.
  - *Resolution:* Built an automated Unicode NFC normalization filter for ZWJ (`U+200D`) and ZWNJ (`U+200C`).
- **Challenge 3: The WiLI-2018 Label Schema Anomaly:**
  - *Problem:* Zero-shot benchmarking initially produced false 0.00 scores for Arabic due to `ara` vs `arb` dictionary omissions.
  - *Resolution:* Conducted an audit of the raw dataset, standardized the BCP-47 mapping, and validated genuine zero-shot performance (0.9985 F1).
- **Core Engineering Takeaway:**
  - *"Bigger is not always better. Deep algorithmic understanding of subword models allowed us to achieve SOTA accuracy at 150x less latency than multi-billion parameter LLMs."*
